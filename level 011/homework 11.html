Grilled Cheese
  • Bread
    •
  • The foundation of the sandwich — sourdough offers tangy flavor and sturdy texture, white bread provides classic softness, and brioche brings a touch of sweetness and richness. Choose bread that is thick enough to stay crispy outside while holding melted cheese inside.

  • Cheese
    •
  • The star ingredient that defines the sandwich. American cheese melts smoothly into creamy perfection, Cheddar adds sharp flavor with good meltability, and Gruyère brings nutty, complex taste. Using 2 slices (about 2 ounces) ensures the perfect bread-to-cheese ratio.

  • Butter
    •
  • Creates the signature golden-brown, crispy crust that makes grilled cheese irresistible. Unsalted butter gives you control over seasoning, while salted butter adds extra flavor. Room temperature butter spreads easily without tearing delicate bread.
